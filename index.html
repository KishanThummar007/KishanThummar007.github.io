I recently completed my master's degree (September 2026). I am interested in robotic simulation, world models and path planning, and in using them to automate wire harness manufacturing and assembly in the automotive industry.

In my master thesis at the Institute FAPS, I developed a MuJoCo framework that plans low-stress, collision-free trajectories and the picking order for loading a wire harness into a multi-gripper while avoiding wire tangling; this work has been accepted at IEEE IEEM 2026. My recent work focuses on benchmarking world models for wire harness handling, comparing world and DLO dynamic models with physics simulators in predicting how cables move on a near-industry setup. In my project thesis, I developed a 3D vision algorithm that locates connector sockets for robotic plugging of a cable harness into a vehicle's centre console.
